HTTP/3 Adoption · Briefing Disco.

QUIC won by request volume,
not by hostname count

  • Request-weighted adoption sits well above site-weighted — the busiest origins flipped the default first [[7]]
  • ~1/3 of top-10M hostnames now offer HTTP/3; the volume served is far higher [[3]]
  • The long tail lags for operational reasons — UDP tuning, middleboxes, missing host toggles — not protocol gaps [[19]]
  • For operators: the request-weighted figure is what predicts real latency wins
